# Law of Uniform Connectedness

> Elements that are visually connected are perceived as more related than elements with no
> connection. (Jon Yablonski, Laws of UX)

Business processes are sequences and relations: a status lifecycle, an order of steps, a control
and the panel it opened. A visible connection explains the relation without a word of text.

## In this collection

- WorkflowStatus draws the lifecycle as one track: done states, the current one as a Badge in its
  tone, the upcoming ones.
- Stepper shows its steps as one numbered list in order, with the current step highlighted.
- Tabs tie the active tab to its content with an accent underline.
- Popover stays anchored to its trigger and follows it while open (D51): the panel visibly
  belongs to the button that opened it, for Menu, FacetFilter, RangePicker and ColumnsPicker.
- Spotlight rings its target with the accent and places the bubble beside it (D25).

## Review questions

- Is every dropdown, bubble and panel on this page visibly tied to what opened it?
- Does each sequence on this page look like one connected sequence?
